Fastshot builds your Android app on its own build machines. You get two files:

- **APK**: the file you install on an Android phone.
- **AAB** (Android App Bundle): the file Google Play needs.

## Prerequisites

- A Starter, Beginner or Pro plan
- For Google Play only: a [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console) account ($25 one-time fee)

## Build from the chat

Ask the AI, for example "build an APK" or "make an Android app file". A build card appears in the chat and shows each step. When the build is done, click **Download APK** or **Download AAB**.

## Build from the Publish view

1. Click **Publish** in the project header. On a phone, open the menu and tap **Publish**.
2. Choose the **Android** tab.
3. Under **App Configuration**, check the **Package Name** and **App Name**, then click **Save Config**. The package name is your app's unique ID, like `com.yourcompany.appname`.
4. Under **Android Builds**, click **Queue Build**.
5. When the build shows **Completed**, click **Download APK** or **Download AAB**.

A build takes several minutes. Without Google Play details, nothing is sent to Google Play: you get the files to download.

## Install the APK on your phone

1. Open the APK on your Android phone.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
3. Tap **Install**.

## Publish on Google Play

1. **Create your app** in [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. **Upload the first build yourself.** Google Play doesn't let a build service upload an app's first build. Download the AAB and upload it in Play Console.
3. **Let later builds upload for you.** Add a Google Play service account: a Google Cloud login that lets Fastshot upload builds. Paste its JSON key under **Google Play Service Account** on the **Android** tab and click **Save Credentials**.
4. **Pick a track and build.** Next to **Queue Build**, choose **Internal**, **Alpha** (closed testing), **Beta** (open testing) or **Production**. The build goes to that track in Play Console.
5. **Complete the store listing** in Play Console and submit it for review.

The service account needs two things in Google:

- The Google Play Android Developer API turned on in its Google Cloud project
- An invitation in Play Console under **Users and permissions**, with release access

Every build gets a higher version code than the one before. Google Play needs that for an update.

<Tip>
  Start with the **Internal** track to check everything works before you release to everyone.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Google Play package names are permanent. If your app is already on Google Play, set **Package Name** to its exact package name before you build.
</Warning>

## Signing key

Android apps must be signed with a key. Fastshot makes one on your first build, and uses it for all your projects. You'll see it under **Android Keystore**.

If your app is already on Google Play with its own key, upload that `.jks` file under **Android Keystore** with its passwords and key alias. If a keystore is already there, click **Replace Keystore** first.

## Store listing tips

- Use clear, descriptive screenshots that show your app's key features
- Write a concise description focusing on what users can do with the app
- Include a privacy policy (required by Google Play)
- Complete the content rating questionnaire accurately

## Troubleshooting

- **Build fails** — the build card shows the reason. Ask the AI to read the build's logs and fix the problem.
- **Only a Download button, no AAB** — that build finished without an AAB. Build again.
- **APK not installing** — allow installs from unknown sources for the app you opened the APK with.
- **"Google Play has no app … that this account can reach yet"** — upload the first AAB in Play Console yourself. For an app already on Google Play, set **Package Name** to its package name.
- **"Your service account has no access to this app"** — invite the service account's email in Play Console under **Users and permissions**, with release access.
- **Missing app icon** — verify the app icon is set in [App Configuration](/working/app-config) before building
