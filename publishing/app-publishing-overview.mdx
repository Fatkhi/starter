Fastshot builds your app as a real Android and iPhone app, on its own build machines. You don't need an Expo account or a GitHub connection to publish.

## The words you'll see

- **APK**: the file you install on an Android phone.
- **AAB** (Android App Bundle): the file Google Play needs.
- **IPA**: the iPhone app file.
- **TestFlight**: Apple's app for trying an iPhone app before it goes on the App Store.

## Publish from the editor

1. Click **Publish** in the project header. On a phone, open the menu and tap **Publish**.
2. The **Deploy** view opens. Choose the **iOS** or the **Android** tab.
3. Follow the steps on the tab, then click **Queue Build**.
4. The build card shows each step while the build runs.
5. When the build is done, download your app file, or find it in Google Play Console or TestFlight.

## Or ask in the chat

Type something like "build an APK" or "make an Android app file I can install". The AI starts the build, and a build card appears in the chat. When the build is done, the card has **Download APK** and **Download AAB** buttons.

If the build needs something first, the card asks for it. Fill it in and click **Build now**.

## Before you publish

Make sure you've:

- Tried the app on your phone: click **Preview on Device** and scan the QR code
- Set the app name and icon in [App Configuration](/working/app-config)
- Connected [Supabase](/integrations/supabase-backend) if your app uses a backend
- Checked that every feature works
